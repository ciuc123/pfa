# YouTube — first 10 videos

YouTube is your distribution channel, not your main business. You start making videos AFTER you have your first client, so each video is proof of what you can do. Don't start before that — you need real results to show.

---

## When to start

Start making videos when:
- You have completed at least one paid engagement (any offer from `strategy/offers/`)
- You have a concrete before/after story to tell

## Your first 10 videos (pick from this list)

Each video should demonstrate the exact paid capability you offer. One video = one proof point.

1. **"I audited a real Laravel codebase — here's what I found"** (Legacy Laravel Audit)
   - Show the codebase structure, the top issues, the prioritized fix plan
   - CTA: "Want me to audit yours? DM me."

2. **"I let AI write tests for a Laravel app — 40% were useless, 20% found real bugs"** (AI Test Coverage Sprint)
   - Show the AI generating tests, running them, which ones pass/fail
   - CTA: "Want me to run this sprint on your codebase? DM me."

3. **"3 N+1 queries that were killing a Laravel app's performance"** (Queue & Performance Review)
   - Show the slow queries, the fix, the before/after response time
   - CTA: "Want a performance review? DM me."

4. **"I cut a Laravel app's cloud bill by 40% — here's what I changed"** (Cloud Cost Audit)
   - Show the billing dashboard, the wasted resources, the savings
   - CTA: "Want a cost audit? DM me."

5. **"I inherited a Laravel project that was on fire — here's my rescue plan"** (Backend Rescue Audit)
   - Walk through the assessment, the prioritized plan, the first fix
   - CTA: "Want a rescue audit? DM me."

6. **"5 things I check first when I look at any Laravel codebase"** (General credibility)
   - Quick checklist video, shows your process
   - CTA: "Want me to check yours? DM me."

7. **"The difference between a senior and mid-level Laravel dev (from 14 years of mistakes)"** (Audience building)
   - Personal experience, builds trust
   - CTA: follow for more

8. **"How I would modernize a 5-year-old Laravel app in 2026"** (Thought leadership)
   - Show your approach to modernization
   - CTA: "Want me to assess yours? DM me."

9. **"I compared 3 AI coding tools on a real Laravel project"** (AI experiments)
   - Show what works and what doesn't
   - CTA: follow for more

10. **"What I learned from my first 5 paid Laravel audits"** (Case study compilation)
    - Summarize patterns from multiple audits
    - CTA: "Want an audit? DM me."

## Format

- 5–10 minutes per video
- Screen recording + voiceover (no need to show your face if you don't want to)
- Use `youtube/video-template.md` to plan each video
- Record in one take, edit lightly (cut dead air, add text overlays for key points)

## Tools

- Recording: OBS (free) or Loom
- Editing: DaVinci Resolve (free) or just YouTube's built-in editor
- Thumbnails: Canva (free)

## Rules

- One video per client engagement (minimum)
- Each video must show something real — real code, real results, real numbers
- No generic "top 10" listicles without real experience behind them
- Post consistently: 1 video per week when you have content, 0 when you don't
- Don't force it — if you don't have a story to tell, wait until you do
