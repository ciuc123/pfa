Story structure

1. Hook — quick curiosity or stakes (5–15 seconds)
2. Setup — context and hypothesis (30–60 seconds)
3. Experiment — show what you did (core footage)
4. Problems / failures — honest issues encountered
5. Result — outcome and evidence
6. What I learned — actionable takeaways
7. Conclusion — next steps and CTA
8. Next video — follow-up ideas

Script (use timestamps and short paragraphs)

- [00:00] Hook
- [00:15] Setup
- [01:00] Experiment start

Recording checklist

- Visuals: screen-recorded terminals, code editors, and architecture diagrams ready
- Audio: microphone test completed, ambient noise minimized
- B-roll: any extra shots or slides prepared
- Clips: recorded small segments for failures and results

Editing checklist

- Trim dead air, remove long pauses
- Insert on-screen highlights (code callouts, zooms)
- Add captions and key takeaways as text overlays
- Ensure clear intro/outro and thumbnail export
