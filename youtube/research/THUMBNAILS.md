# Thumbnail Knowledge Base

Principles

- One clear idea: a single visual concept should be readable at small sizes.
- High contrast: strong foreground/background separation.
- Minimal text: only the essential words, complementing the title.
- Visual curiosity: evoke a question or momentum.
- Complement the title rather than duplicate it.
- Recognizable subject: make it obvious who or what the video is about.
- Avoid clutter and small icons.

Testing thumbnails

Keep a log of experiments and results here (do not invent metrics). Record:

- Date
- Video (title)
- Thumbnail variation (A/B if applicable)
- Observations (CTR, audience comments)
- Notes on follow-ups

Example checklist when designing a thumbnail

1. Does it read at small sizes?
2. Is there a single focal point?
3. Is the text short and bold?
4. Does it create curiosity without lying?

Tools and quick workflow

- Rapid mockups: Canva / GIMP
- Keep a master PSD or layered file for reusable assets (not committed here).
