# Monetization

Possible revenue streams (initial list)

1. YouTube advertising (AdSense)
2. Affiliate commissions (developer tools, hosting, courses)
3. Sponsorships (tools and services aligned with the audience)
4. Digital products (guides, templates, starter kits)
5. Developer tools / templates (Laravel starters, infra scripts)
6. SaaS (only after clear audience demand and validation)
7. Consulting / architecture reviews
8. Freelance leads
9. Courses / paid guides (after proven demand)

Principles

- The goal is diversification: do not treat AdSense as the only viable income source.
- Do not build a product before there is evidence that people want it. Prioritize validation and audience feedback.
- Monetization should follow audience needs and trust, not force-fit products into content.

Milestone scenarios (aspirational planning — not forecasts)

- €0 → early validation
- €100/month → small affiliate / ad income
- €500/month → repeatable affiliate/sponsorships or a small product
- €1,500/month → steady mix of ads, affiliate income and occasional consulting
- €3,000/month → multiple small recurring products or regular consulting + sponsorships
- €5,000+/month → product or SaaS with recurring revenue

These are planning milestones to help think about resource allocation — label them as scenarios, not guarantees.

Distribution path

Audience → trust (consistent, useful experiments) → distribution (playlist & formats) → monetization (soft offers, affiliate links, paid guides, consulting)

Validation checklist before product building

1. Repeated requests or comments from viewers for similar solutions.
2. Hundreds (not tens) of engaged viewers demonstrating demand for a specific help area.
3. Successful pilots (free or low-cost) that show willingness to pay.

Short-term next steps for monetization

- Add unobtrusive links to recommended tools in video descriptions.
- Track audience questions that could indicate product demand (collect in `products/IDEAS.md`).
- Test small paid offers (one-page guide, template) only after clear evidence.
