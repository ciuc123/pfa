# North Star

What I'm trying to build

- A small, sustainable English-language YouTube media asset built around my existing technical expertise (backend development, Laravel/PHP, AWS/cloud, AI-assisted engineering). The channel's primary outcome is distribution and a trusting audience that can later be converted into multiple revenue streams.

Why YouTube

- Video is the highest-leverage medium for technical demonstrations and experiments: it combines demonstration, narration and screen recording in a single asset that can be discovered and reused.
- YouTube provides built-in distribution and a persistent audience discovery engine that, when paired with repeatable formats, can amplify product ideas and consulting opportunities.

Long-term business model (high level)

Audience → Trust → Distribution → Multiple monetization streams (ads, affiliates, sponsorships, products, consulting, SaaS/templates).

Creator advantages

- 14+ years backend experience and real-world projects to show.
- Deep Laravel/PHP knowledge — useful for a focused technical niche but not limiting the broader developer audience.
- Cloud / infrastructure experience (AWS, Docker) and practical product-building experience.
- Real ability to run experiments, build prototypes and interpret results.

Channel personality

- Face-optional but personal: personality comes from my voice, judgment, mistakes and real projects.
- Curious, experimental, skeptical of marketing hype, results-driven.

Content philosophy (summary)

Prioritize: 1) real experiment, 2) personal story, 3) evidence-backed opinion, 4) practical explanation, 5) tutorial.

Desired viewer perception

- "This person actually builds things and tests them." 
- "They don't just repeat marketing claims — they show what happened." 
- "I want to see what they try next."
