# Title Patterns & Experimentation

This document is a running knowledge base of title patterns to test. These are patterns to experiment with, not formulas that guarantee success.

Common patterns

- I Tried X
- I Gave X to Y
- Can X Actually Y?
- X vs Y
- I Let X Do Y
- I Built X
- I Replaced X With Y
- What Happens When X?
- I Tried X for 30 Days

Channel-relevant examples

- "I Let 3 AI Agents Build the Same Laravel API"
- "I Gave Codex a Real Laravel Project"
- "Can AI Understand a 10-Year-Old PHP Codebase?"
- "I Tried Running a SaaS for €0/month"
- "10 Laravel Mistakes I Don't Make Anymore"

Notes on testing titles

1. A/B test titles where possible (YouTube experiments or different titles on social shares).
2. Track CTR and retention to see which patterns correlate with watch time.
3. Avoid misleading language; prioritize curiosity with truthfulness.

Title checklist before publishing

- Is there a clear stake or surprise in the title?
- Could someone click this without knowing me?
- Is the title specific enough to imply an experiment or outcome?
