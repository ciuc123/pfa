#+ Competitor Research Framework

This file is a structured template for researching competitor channels. Do NOT record invented metrics here — use public channel pages and your notes.

Fields (table-friendly)

- Channel
- URL
- Niche
- Audience (who they target)
- Recurring formats (e.g., experiments, tutorials, listicles)
- Title patterns
- Thumbnail patterns
- Apparent strengths
- Apparent weaknesses
- Interesting ideas to adapt (not copy)
- Things NOT to copy
- Notes

Competitor research rules

1. Purpose: understand the market, not copy successful creators.
2. Record only observable facts (titles, formats, cadence) and your interpretation separately in Notes.
3. Do not duplicate content; identify gaps or formats that could be adapted with original experiments.
4. Capture inspiration and concrete lessons (e.g., distinctive thumbnail treatments, recurring segment ideas).

How to use

- Add one entry per channel you study. Keep the list focused (10–20 relevant channels at most).
- Periodically review whether any format maps to your pillars and experiment with adapted versions.
