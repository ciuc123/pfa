# YouTube

YouTube is your distribution channel. It proves your expertise and drives inbound leads. But it comes AFTER LinkedIn content and your first client — not before.

**Start here when you're ready: [first-10-videos.md](first-10-videos.md)**

## The content sequence (Gary V approach)

1. **LinkedIn first (now):** Post 2–3 times per week. Document your journey — what you're learning, patterns you see, mistakes you make. This is the fastest feedback loop. See `business/03-weekly-workflow.md`.
2. **YouTube second (after first client):** Turn client case studies into videos. Each video = proof of what you can deliver. See `first-10-videos.md`.
3. **YouTube ongoing (after 5+ videos):** Start experimenting with broader content — AI coding experiments, Laravel opinions, developer business tips.

## What you need for YouTube

- `first-10-videos.md` — your first 10 video ideas, each tied to a paid offer
- `video-template.md` — fill this in before recording each video
- `learnings.md` — record what works and what doesn't after each video

## Rules

- One video per client engagement (minimum)
- Each video shows real code, real results, real numbers
- No generic listicles without real experience behind them
- 5–10 minutes per video, screen recording + voiceover
- Don't start until you have a real before/after story to tell
