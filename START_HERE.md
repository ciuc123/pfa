## Anti-overthinking rules (read these first)

1. **You are not choosing forever.** Every decision here is for a 30-day test. If it doesn't work, you change it.
2. **Bad data beats no data.** 30 messages with a "good enough" offer beats 0 messages with a perfect offer.
3. **The goal is 30 messages, not the perfect offer.** You learn by sending, not by planning.
4. **If you're stuck, pick the easiest option.** The buyer group you can reach fastest. The offer you can deliver most confidently. Start there.
5. **Done is better than perfect.** Ship the message. Fix it later.
6. **You are not a beginner.** You have 15 years of PHP experience, 10+ years with Laravel, and 6 years of freelance work. Your problem is lead flow and packaging, not ability.

## Step 0 — Warm network sprint (first 7 days, do this FIRST)

Before cold outreach, tap your existing network. This is your fastest path to cash.

Read: [`business/current-experiment.md`](business/current-experiment.md) for the full sprint plan.

1. List 20 warm contacts: old freelance clients, ex-colleagues, agency owners, founders, recruiters who know freelance buyers
2. Message them: "I'm productizing my Laravel/AWS/backend consulting into fixed-scope audits. Looking for 2–3 teams with messy Laravel apps, performance issues, or deployment risk. Do you know anyone?"
3. Separately message 10 agencies/dev shops on LinkedIn offering senior Laravel overflow / audit support
4. Use your existing content (Laravel 11 post, PHP 8.4 post, AI agents post) as free value assets
5. Only AFTER warm outreach, start cold LinkedIn outreach (Step 1+)

This is more likely to create short-term revenue than pure cold outreach.
