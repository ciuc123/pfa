# START HERE

This is the only file you need to read to get started. Follow these steps in order. Do not read the other files until you reach the step that tells you to.

---

## Step 1 — Pick who you sell to

Read: [`business/01-pick-icp.md`](business/01-pick-icp.md)

Score the 5 candidate buyer groups. Pick the highest-scoring one. Write your one-line ICP at the bottom of that file.

Time: 30–45 minutes.

---

## Step 2 — Check that 30 prospects exist

Read: [`business/02-validate-market.md`](business/02-validate-market.md)

Search LinkedIn for 30 prospects who match your ICP and show a visible buying signal. Time yourself. If it takes less than 2 hours, your ICP is valid. If not, go back to Step 1 and pick a different buyer group.

Time: up to 2 hours.

---

## Step 3 — Pick one offer

Read: [`strategy/offers/README.md`](strategy/offers/README.md)

Do NOT read every offer. Skim the table of offers, pick 1 or 2 that match your ICP's pain. Open only those. Fill in the "Can I deliver this?" checklist honestly.

Time: 20–30 minutes.

---

## Step 4 — Write your outreach messages

Read: [`strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md`](strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md)

Pick the signal type that matches your ICP. Write 2 message variants using the signal as the first line. Save them in the validation tracker.

Time: 20 minutes.

---

## Step 5 — Send 30 messages

Use: [`strategy/validation-tracker.csv`](strategy/validation-tracker.csv)

Research 30 prospects, add them to the tracker, send your 2 message variants (15 each). Track which variant gets more replies.

Time: 3–5 days (spread across the week).

---

## Step 6 — Review and decide

Review your tracker. Count: messages sent, replies received, meetings booked.

- If you got 2+ meetings: your ICP and offer work. Move to the weekly workflow.
- If you got 0 meetings: go back to Step 1 and pick a different buyer group or offer.

Read: [`business/03-weekly-workflow.md`](business/03-weekly-workflow.md)

---

## After Step 6

Once you have a validated ICP and offer, your weekly loop is:

1. Research and message 10 prospects/day (Step 5, ongoing)
2. Review tracker weekly
3. When you get a client, deliver the offer, then turn the experience into a YouTube video

YouTube comes after you have your first client. Not before. When you're ready: [`youtube/first-10-videos.md`](youtube/first-10-videos.md)
