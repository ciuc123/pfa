# START HERE

This is the only file you need to read. Follow the steps in order. Don't read other files until a step tells you to.

---

## Anti-overthinking rules (read these first)

1. **You are not choosing forever.** Every decision here is for a 30-day test. If it doesn't work, you change it.
2. **Bad data beats no data.** 30 messages with a "good enough" offer beats 0 messages with a perfect offer.
3. **The goal is 30 messages, not the perfect offer.** You learn by sending, not by planning.
4. **If you're stuck, pick the easiest option.** The buyer group you can reach fastest. The offer you can deliver most confidently. Start there.
5. **Done is better than perfect.** Ship the message. Fix it later.

---

## Step 1 — Pick who you sell to

Read: [`business/01-pick-icp.md`](business/01-pick-icp.md)

Score the 5 candidate buyer groups. Pick the highest-scoring one. Write your one-line ICP.

Time: 30–45 minutes.

---

## Step 2 — Check that 30 prospects exist

Read: [`business/02-validate-market.md`](business/02-validate-market.md)

Search LinkedIn for 30 prospects who match your ICP and show a visible buying signal. If it takes less than 2 hours, your ICP is valid. If not, go back to Step 1.

Time: up to 2 hours.

---

## Step 3 — Pick one offer

Read: [`strategy/offers/README.md`](strategy/offers/README.md)

Skim the table. Pick 1–2 offers that match your ICP's pain. Fill in the "Can I deliver this?" checklist. Pick ONE.

Then read: [`strategy/value-ladder.md`](strategy/value-ladder.md) to understand how your offer fits in the free → entry → core → ongoing path.

Time: 20–30 minutes.

---

## Step 4 — Lock in your experiment

Read: [`business/current-experiment.md`](business/current-experiment.md)

Fill in: your chosen ICP, your chosen offer, your 2 outreach message variants, and your success metric. This is your commitment for the next 30 days.

Time: 20 minutes.

---

## Step 5 — Prepare your free value asset

Read: [`strategy/free-value-assets.md`](strategy/free-value-assets.md)

Pick ONE free asset (checklist, Loom, repo teardown, job post teardown). You'll send this BEFORE asking for anything. This is your "jab."

Time: 15 minutes to prepare the asset.

---

## Step 6 — Send 30 messages

Use: [`strategy/validation-tracker.csv`](strategy/validation-tracker.csv)

Research 30 prospects. For each one:
1. Find a buying signal (see [`strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md`](strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md))
2. Send your free value asset first
3. Follow up using the sequence in [`business/03-weekly-workflow.md`](business/03-weekly-workflow.md)
4. Track everything in the validation tracker

Send 15 of variant A and 15 of variant B. See which gets more replies.

Time: 3–5 days (spread across the week).

---

## Step 7 — Run discovery calls

Read: [`business/04-discovery-call.md`](business/04-discovery-call.md)

When someone replies, book a 15–20 minute call. Use the 6-question script. Don't sell — listen. If they're in pain and have urgency, pitch your offer.

Time: 15–20 minutes per call.

---

## Step 8 — Get your first 3 clients

Read: [`business/first-3-clients.md`](business/first-3-clients.md)

Your first 3 clients are about proof, not money. Client 1: free or €100–200. Client 2: €300–500. Client 3: €500–1,000. Each one gives you a testimonial, before/after metrics, and a case study.

After client 3, raise your price to full entry price.

---

## Step 9 — Review and decide

Review your tracker and `business/current-experiment.md`:
- Messages sent, replies received, meetings booked, clients signed
- If you got 2+ meetings: your ICP and offer work. Keep going.
- If you got 0 meetings after 3 weeks: go back to Step 1, pick a new buyer group or offer.

---

## After Step 9: your weekly loop

Once you have a validated ICP and offer, every week is:

1. Research and message 10 prospects/day (Step 6, ongoing)
2. Follow up with non-repliers (sequence in `03-weekly-workflow.md`)
3. Run discovery calls when you get replies (`04-discovery-call.md`)
4. Deliver the offer when you get a client (use the offer doc's methodology)
5. Collect proof from every client (`first-3-clients.md`)
6. Post 2–3 things on LinkedIn per week about what you're learning
7. Review tracker every Friday and update `current-experiment.md`

YouTube comes after you have your first client case study. When you're ready: [`youtube/first-10-videos.md`](youtube/first-10-videos.md)
