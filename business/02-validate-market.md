# Step 2 — Validate your market (the 30-prospect test)

Before committing to your ICP, you need to confirm that real prospects exist and are findable. This test takes up to 2 hours. If it takes longer, your ICP is too narrow or too hard to reach.

---

## What you need

- Your one-line ICP from `01-pick-icp.md`
- A LinkedIn account (Sales Navigator optional but helpful)
- A spreadsheet or `strategy/validation-tracker.csv` to log prospects

---

## The test

### 1. Search for prospects (up to 2 hours)

Use LinkedIn search or Sales Navigator to find 30 people who match BOTH:
- Your chosen buyer role (e.g. "CTO", "Head of Engineering", "agency owner")
- A visible buying signal (e.g. hiring a Laravel developer, posting about legacy code, just raised funding)

Use the signal types from [`strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md`](../strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md) to guide your search.

### 2. For each prospect, record:

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| Name | Full name |
| Role | Job title |
| Company | Company name |
| Signal | The exact thing you noticed (copy the wording) |
| Signal source | Where you found it (LinkedIn job post, blog post, etc.) |
| Message variant | A or B (you'll write these in Step 4) |
| Status | Found / Messaged / Replied / Meeting booked |

### 3. Decision

- **30 prospects found in under 2 hours:** Your ICP is valid. Move to Step 3.
- **Fewer than 30, or took more than 2 hours:** Go back to `01-pick-icp.md`, pick your second-highest buyer group, and repeat this test.

---

## Tips

- Start with the strongest signal type: hiring. It's the easiest to search for.
- Use boolean search: `"head of engineering" AND laravel` or `"CTO" AND "SaaS" AND laravel`
- Don't message anyone yet. This step is only about confirming the pool exists.
- If you find 30+ quickly, you have room to be more specific (narrow the ICP further).
