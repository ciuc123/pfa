# First 3 clients playbook — getting proof when you're already experienced

You have 14 years of experience and 6 years of freelance work. You are NOT starting from zero. Your problem is lead flow and packaging, not ability. Your first 3 productized clients are about proving the productized format works — not about proving you can code.

---

## The plan

### Client 1: Founder/pilot price

- **Who:** A warm contact or former freelance client who has a Laravel project
- **Price:** €500–750 (founder price — discounted in exchange for proof)
- **What you deliver:** The full audit (same offer you picked in Step 3)
- **What you ask for in return:**
  - [ ] A written testimonial (I'll write it, they approve it)
  - [ ] Permission to use before/after metrics (anonymized if needed)
  - [ ] A screenshot of the audit report (with sensitive info removed)
  - [ ] A LinkedIn recommendation (they post it on your profile)
  - [ ] Permission to turn it into a YouTube video (anonymized)
  - [ ] A referral to 1 other person who might need this

### Client 2: Near-market price

- **Who:** A cold prospect from your outreach (found via buying signals)
- **Price:** €750–1,200
- **What you deliver:** Same audit
- **What you ask for in return:**
  - [ ] Testimonial
  - [ ] Before/after metrics
  - [ ] Case study permission

### Client 3: Full price

- **Who:** Another prospect (warm or cold)
- **Price:** €1,200–1,500
- **What you deliver:** Same audit
- **What you ask for in return:**
  - [ ] Testimonial
  - [ ] Before/after metrics
  - [ ] Case study permission
  - [ ] Referral

---

## After client 3

You now have:
- 3 testimonials
- 3 before/after metrics
- 3 case studies
- 3 YouTube video ideas
- Referrals

Now you raise your price to €1,500+ for the audit. Now your outreach messages say: "I helped [type of company] find [specific risk] and fix it in [timeframe]. Want me to do the same for you?"

---

## How to get client 1 this week

You already have a network. Use it:

1. Open your phone/contacts/LinkedIn connections.
2. Find 10 people who: (a) work with Laravel/PHP, (b) have a codebase, (c) you have some relationship with. Think: former freelance clients, ex-colleagues who moved to other companies, agency owners you've talked to.
3. Message them: "Hey [name], I'm productizing my Laravel/AWS backend consulting into fixed-scope audits. I'm looking for 2–3 teams with messy Laravel apps, performance issues, or deployment risk. I'll do a full audit for €500 (normally €1,500) in exchange for a testimonial and a case study. Know anyone who'd benefit?"
4. Pick the first one who says yes.
5. Deliver the audit using the methodology in your offer doc.
6. Get the testimonial and metrics.

---

## Positioning rule

**Never position yourself as cheap help. Position yourself as senior risk reduction.**

Your 14 years and 6 years of freelance experience are your differentiator. A €500 audit from a 14-year senior is a steal. A €500 audit from a junior is a risk. Make sure prospects know which one you are.
