# First 3 clients playbook — how to get proof when you have none

You have zero testimonials, zero case studies, and zero proof. This is normal. Every business starts here. Hormozi's advice: borrow credibility or create it by doing the work for free or cheap, then turn it into proof.

Your first 3 clients are not about money. They are about proof.

---

## The plan

### Client 1: Free or heavily discounted

- **Who:** A warm contact, former colleague, or someone in your network who has a Laravel project
- **Price:** Free or €100–200 (just to have a transaction)
- **What you deliver:** The full audit (same offer you picked in Step 3)
- **What you ask for in return:**
  - [ ] A written testimonial (I'll write it, they approve it)
  - [ ] Permission to use before/after metrics (anonymized if needed)
  - [ ] A screenshot of the audit report (with sensitive info removed)
  - [ ] A LinkedIn recommendation (they post it on your profile)
  - [ ] Permission to turn it into a YouTube video (anonymized)

### Client 2: Discounted

- **Who:** A cold prospect from your outreach (found via buying signals)
- **Price:** €300–500 (50% of your normal price)
- **What you deliver:** Same audit
- **What you ask for in return:**
  - [ ] Testimonial
  - [ ] Before/after metrics
  - [ ] Permission for case study (anonymized)

### Client 3: Near full price

- **Who:** Another cold prospect
- **Price:** €500–1,000 (approaching your normal price)
- **What you deliver:** Same audit
- **What you ask for in return:**
  - [ ] Testimonial
  - [ ] Before/after metrics
  - [ ] Case study permission
  - [ ] Referral to 1 other person who might need this

---

## After client 3

You now have:
- 3 testimonials
- 3 before/after metrics
- 3 case studies
- 3 YouTube video ideas
- 1 referral

Now you raise your price to full entry price (€500–1,500). Now you have proof. Now your outreach messages can say: "I helped [type of company] find [specific risk] and fix it in [timeframe]. Want me to do the same for you?"

---

## How to get client 1 this week

1. Open your phone/contacts/LinkedIn connections.
2. Find 5 people who: (a) work with Laravel/PHP, (b) have a codebase, (c) you have some relationship with.
3. Message them: "Hey [name], I'm starting a productized service doing Laravel codebase audits. I'm looking for 3 people to do a free audit for in exchange for a testimonial. You'd get a full audit report + a 30-day fix plan. Interested?"
4. Pick the first one who says yes.
5. Deliver the audit using the methodology in your offer doc.
6. Get the testimonial and metrics.

This is your fastest path to proof. Don't skip it. Don't wait for the "perfect" client. Get one, deliver, get proof.
