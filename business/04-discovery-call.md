# Step 4 — The discovery call script

Someone replied to your message. Now what? This is the call that turns a reply into a client. Keep it short (15–20 minutes). Don't sell. Listen.

---

## Before the call

- Read your tracker: what signal did you find? What free asset did you send?
- Have your offer doc open (the one you picked in Step 3)
- Have a calendar link ready (Calendly, Cal.com, or just propose 2 time slots)

---

## The call (15–20 minutes)

### Opening (2 minutes)

"Thanks for taking the time, [First name]. I know you're busy, so I'll keep this short. I sent you [the checklist / Loom / risk report] — was that useful at all?"

Listen. Let them talk.

### The 6 questions (10 minutes)

Ask these in order. Write down their answers.

1. **Current problem:** "What's the biggest thing slowing your team down right now with [their codebase / infra / testing]?"
2. **Cost of the problem:** "What happens if you don't fix this? How much time / money / frustration is it costing you?"
3. **Urgency:** "Is this something you want to fix this quarter, or is it more of a 'someday' thing?"
4. **Current workaround:** "What have you tried so far? Anyone on the team already looking at this?"
5. **Decision process:** "If we decide to work together, who else needs to be involved in the decision?"
6. **Success criteria:** "What would a good outcome look like for you? If I could deliver [specific thing] in [timeframe], would that be valuable?"

### The pitch (3 minutes)

Only if their answers show real pain and urgency. If they're not in pain, don't pitch — offer to stay in touch.

"Based on what you've told me, here's what I'd do: [name the offer]. In [timeframe], I'd deliver [specific outcome]. The investment is [price]. Does that sound like it would help?"

### Close (2 minutes)

- If yes: "Great. I'll send you a quick summary and a payment link. Can we start on [date]?"
- If maybe: "No pressure. I'll send you the summary so you have it. Ping me whenever you're ready."
- If no: "Totally understand. Mind if I check back in a few weeks?"

## After the call

- [ ] Add notes to `strategy/validation-tracker.csv` (meeting outcome, next action)
- [ ] Send a follow-up message within 2 hours: "Thanks for the call. Here's a quick summary of what we discussed: [3 bullet points]. Next step: [payment link / proposal / whatever you agreed]."
- [ ] If they said yes — congratulations, you have your first client. Go to `business/first-3-clients.md`.
