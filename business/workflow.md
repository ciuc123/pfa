# Business Workflow — daily / weekly / monthly

Purpose

- A prescriptive, timeboxed checklist of tasks you should complete to run the productized service while using YouTube for distribution. Follow this for each week during the 90‑day test.

Operating principle

- [ ] Time split suggestion (adjust after product definition): Product delivery ~60% of working time; Content & experiments ~40%.
- [ ] If time is constrained, drop content cadence to 1 short/week and keep outreach consistent.

Daily routine (timeboxes & exact steps)

-- Morning (30–60 mins) — Outreach & discovery
  - [ ] Research 8–12 prospects (boolean searches / signals) and add to `strategy/TRACKING_SHEET.csv`.
  - [ ] Send outreach batch (use message variant A/B from `strategy/OUTBOUND_MESSAGES.md`).
  - [ ] Reply to any inbound messages and triage meeting requests.

-- Midday (60–180 mins) — Product delivery / client work
  - [ ] Complete the week's top client deliverable(s) (per `strategy/OFFER.md` schedule).
  - [ ] Capture any interesting outcomes, quotes or metrics to use later as case-study material.

-- Afternoon / creative slot (60–120 mins) — Content & experiments (2× weekly recommended)
  - [ ] Day for scripting/recording: use `youtube/Create.md` as your workflow template.
  - [ ] Publish small experiments / shorts: 1–2 shorts per week (or 1 longer video every 1–2 weeks depending on time).
  - [ ] Add experiment notes to `youtube/research/LEARNINGS.md`.

-- End of day (10–15 mins) — housekeeping
  - [ ] Update `strategy/TRACKING_SHEET.csv` with replies / meeting outcomes.
  - [ ] Note one learning and one action for tomorrow (append to `strategy/NOTES.md` or `youtube/research/LEARNINGS.md`).

Weekly tasks (pick a fixed day/time for review)

- [ ] Outreach batch: research + message 30 prospects in a 3‑day test or schedule regular weekly batches.
- [ ] Publish cadence: at least 1 short or post derived from client work; if possible publish 1 longer video bi-weekly.
- [ ] Metrics review (30–60 minutes): open `strategy/TRACKING_SHEET.csv` and `youtube/analytics/` and record:
   - Messages sent, replies %, meetings booked, meetings→close %, revenue closed this week
   - Videos published, views, watch time, followers/ subscribers change, leads generated from videos
- [ ] Decide one experiment for next week (messaging tweak or video format tweak).

Monthly tasks

- [ ] Offer & pricing check: compare close rates across price points; adjust or run price A/B if needed.
- [ ] Case study generation: turn each closed client into at least one short YouTube case study and one LinkedIn post (anonymise as needed). Store in `youtube/case-studies/`.
- [ ] Capacity planning: if demand exceeds capacity, plan a handoff or hire (virtual assistant / part-time appointment setter).

90‑day review (decision rubric)

- [ ] Evaluate against targets:
  - [ ] Revenue: did we reach ~€10,000 in sales over 90 days? (if not, did we hit a smaller but repeatable number?)
  - [ ] Repeatability: do we have a documented onboarding + product delivery that can be handed off?
  - [ ] Leads: are video CTAs producing inbound leads (N leads/month)?
- [ ] Decision gate: If revenue + repeatability hit threshold → scale product operations; otherwise, reallocate time toward the weakest axis (outreach or content).

Product brainstorming session (preparation + sprint)

- [ ] Duration: 90 minutes (preferred) or two 45-minute sessions.
- [ ] Inputs to prepare (30 minutes before session):
  - [ ] 5 customer pain notes (LinkedIn posts, job posts, support notes).
  - [ ] 3 outcome ideas (what result we can guarantee or measure).
  - [ ] Any relevant client numbers or proof points.
- [ ] Agenda (90 minutes):
  - [ ] 10m — Quick context & North Star recap.
  - [ ] 20m — Problem mapping: discuss 5 customer problems and rank by impact and urgency.
  - [ ] 20m — Solution sketch: propose 4 product deliverables and pick top 2.
  - [ ] 20m — Pricing & onboarding: pick price test points and what Week 0 onboarding contains.
  - [ ] 15m — Experiment plan: how to test the product in the next 30 days (outbound cohort, landing page, one pilot client).
  - [ ] 5m — Assign owners and deadlines.

How YouTube maps to product experiments

-- Publish one short/mini-case per client milestone (Week 2 outcome, Week 6 result, Week 12 close). Each video should include a one-line CTA (DM / calendar link / landing page).
  - [ ] Film short behind-the-scenes clips of delivery to show concrete value and build trust.

Where to store outputs

- Product notes & brainstorm results → `strategy/` (create `strategy/brainstorm/` or `strategy/NOTES.md`).
- Video scripts & experiments → `youtube/content/`, `youtube/experiments/` and `youtube/research/LEARNINGS.md`.
- Case studies → `youtube/case-studies/`.


Quick weekly checklist (to pin to your calendar)

- [ ] Monday morning: Outreach research + 10–30 messages.
- [ ] Wednesday: Publish short or edit recorded footage.
- [ ] Friday: Weekly metrics review (30–60m) + plan 1 experiment for next week.

Notes

- Keep the workflow tight and measurable. The daily outreach and weekly review are the gearbox that turns experiments into revenue.
