# Step 3 — Weekly workflow

Once you've validated your ICP and offer (Steps 1–2), this is your weekly loop. Follow it every week. Keep it simple.

---

## Daily (30–60 minutes)

- [ ] Research 8–10 prospects and add them to `strategy/validation-tracker.csv`
- [ ] Send outreach messages (use your A/B variants from Step 4)
- [ ] Reply to any inbound messages
- [ ] Update the tracker with any replies or meeting outcomes

## Weekly (pick one fixed day, e.g. Friday)

- [ ] Review the tracker: how many messages sent, replies, meetings booked this week?
- [ ] Pick the top-performing message variant. Use it more next week.
- [ ] If you got a meeting this week, plan what to say on the call (use the offer doc as your guide)
- [ ] Note one learning (what worked, what didn't)

## When you get a client

- [ ] Deliver the offer (follow the day-by-day methodology in the offer doc)
- [ ] After delivery, write a short summary of what you did and the outcome
- [ ] Turn that summary into a YouTube video idea (see `youtube/first-10-videos.md`)

## When you have 0 meetings after 2 weeks

- [ ] Go back to `01-pick-icp.md` and check your scoring
- [ ] Try a different signal type (see `strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md`)
- [ ] If still nothing after 3 weeks, pick a different buyer group or offer

---

## Time allocation

- Outreach and research: ~60% of your working time
- Delivering client work (when you have a client): ~40%
- YouTube content: only after you have your first client case study
