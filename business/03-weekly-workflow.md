# Step 3 — Weekly workflow

Once you've validated your ICP and offer (Steps 1–2), this is your weekly loop. Follow it every week. Keep it simple.

---

## Daily (30–60 minutes)

- [ ] Research 8–10 prospects and add them to `strategy/validation-tracker.csv`
- [ ] Send a free value asset first (see `strategy/free-value-assets.md`) — THEN ask for a meeting
- [ ] Send outreach messages (use your A/B variants from `business/current-experiment.md`)
- [ ] Follow up with non-repliers (see follow-up sequence below)
- [ ] Reply to any inbound messages
- [ ] Update the tracker with any replies or meeting outcomes

## Follow-up sequence (for every prospect who doesn't reply)

Don't send one message and give up. Follow this sequence:

| When | What to send |
|---|---|
| **Day 0** | Free value asset (checklist, Loom, risk report) + soft micro-ask |
| **Day 3** | Follow-up: "Did you get a chance to look at [the checklist / video]? Happy to answer any questions." |
| **Day 7** | One concrete example: "I just did an audit for a similar team and found [specific thing]. Want me to check yours?" |
| **Day 14** | Close the loop: "No rush on this. If it's not the right time, no worries. I'll check back in a month." |

If they reply at any point, stop the sequence and move to the discovery call (`business/04-discovery-call.md`).

## Weekly (pick one fixed day, e.g. Friday)

- [ ] Review the tracker: how many messages sent, replies, meetings booked this week?
- [ ] Pick the top-performing message variant. Use it more next week.
- [ ] Update `business/current-experiment.md` with the week's numbers
- [ ] If you got a meeting this week, prepare using `business/04-discovery-call.md`
- [ ] Note one learning (what worked, what didn't) in `youtube/learnings.md`
- [ ] Post 1 thing on LinkedIn about what you learned this week (see "LinkedIn content" below)

## LinkedIn content (Gary V: document, don't create)

Post on LinkedIn 2–3 times per week. Don't wait for YouTube. Don't wait for case studies. Document the journey:

- What you learned researching prospects this week
- A Laravel tip or observation from your audit work
- A mistake you made and what you learned
- A pattern you noticed in codebases you've looked at
- Your progress: "Week 2 of 30: sent 20 messages, got 3 replies, booked 1 call"

This is "jab, jab, jab, right hook" — give value 3 times, then ask once. Your posts build trust so that when you do reach out, people recognize you.

## When you get a client

- [ ] Deliver the offer (follow the day-by-day methodology in the offer doc)
- [ ] Follow `business/first-3-clients.md` to collect testimonial + metrics
- [ ] After delivery, write a short summary of what you did and the outcome
- [ ] Turn that summary into a YouTube video idea (see `youtube/first-10-videos.md`)

## When you have 0 meetings after 2 weeks

- [ ] Go back to `01-pick-icp.md` and check your scoring
- [ ] Try a different free value asset (see `strategy/free-value-assets.md`)
- [ ] Try a different signal type (see `strategy/BUYING_SIGNALS.md`)
- [ ] If still nothing after 3 weeks, pick a different buyer group or offer

---

## Time allocation

- Outreach and research: ~50% of your working time
- Delivering client work (when you have a client): ~30%
- LinkedIn content: ~10%
- Review and learning: ~10%
- YouTube content: only after you have your first client case study
