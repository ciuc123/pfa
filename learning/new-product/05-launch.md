# Phase 5: Launch — Scaling to Your First 10 Customers

**Duration:** 10–30 days
**Goal:** Systematically land 5–7 more customers and build proof of repeatability
**Output:** 10 total customers + case studies + testimonials + repeatable sales process

---

## Why This Phase Matters

You've proven you can deliver. Now you're learning to sell repeatedly. This phase is about:

1. **Selling systematically** (not waiting for luck)
2. **Building social proof** (case studies, testimonials, metrics)
3. **Improving your sales messaging** (what messaging works?)
4. **Refining your delivery** (how fast can you deliver?)

By the end, you'll have a repeatable system: outreach → call → close → deliver → proof.

## Exercise 2: Refine Your Sales Message

**Time: 1–2 hours**

After 3 deliveries, you know more about your customer and what resonates.

Look at what worked in your outreach:
- Who replied? Why?
- What specific problem did the closing customers mention?
- What was their biggest pain?
- What made them decide to say yes?

**Create 3 messaging variations:**

```
**Variation 1: The Problem-First Message**
"Hi [Name], I saw your team is dealing with [specific problem]. I've helped 3 agencies in similar situations. Quick question: ___?"

**Variation 2: The Proof-First Message**
"Hi [Name], I just helped [similar company] solve [specific problem] and cut their [metric] by 60%. Your company looks like it might be facing the same issue. How are you handling [problem]?"

"Hi [Name], [existing customer] mentioned your team might be dealing with [specific problem]. They had great results with me. Worth a 10-minute chat?"

## Exercise 3: Create a Simple Sales Funnel

**Time: 2 hours**

You need a repeatable system for turning prospects into customers.

**The sales funnel:**

```
Prospect List (20 warm, 10+ cold) → Message → Reply → Call → Close → Deliver → Proof
```

**Here's the step-by-step:**

1. **Message (Day 1)**
   - Send your best-performing message variation
   - Personalize with specific detail about them/their company
   - Goal: get a reply

2. **Reply engagement (Day 2–5)**
   - If they reply: ask your problem confirmation questions
   - If they seem interested: send calendar link for a call
   - If no reply after 5 days: send ONE follow-up message

3. **Call (Day 6–10)**
   - 20–30 minute conversation
   - Goal: confirm the problem + understand budget
   - If both exist: pitch your product
   - If yes: send invoice and next steps

4. **Invoice (Day 11–12)**
   - Simple invoice with service name + price
   - Set start date (3–5 days out)
   - Payment terms (7 days)

5. **Deliver (Week 2–4)**
   - Execute your process (documented in Phase 4)
   - Stay on timeline
   - Document the results

6. **Proof (Day 1 after delivery)**
   - Send testimonial request
   - Ask for metrics
   - Plan case study

**Funnel template (track every prospect):**

```

|----------|---------------|--------|-----------|---------|----------------|---------|

## Exercise 4: Run Your Sales Process

**Time: 1–3 hours per day for 14–30 days**

Now execute the sales funnel:

**Daily routine:**

- **Message 3–5 new warm prospects** (from your Phase 2 list or referrals)
- **Follow up with 2–3 people** who got your message but didn't reply
- **Book calls with anyone ready** for conversations
- **Run 1–2 sales calls** per day
- **Track everything** in your funnel spreadsheet

**Sales call script (20 minutes):**

```
**Opener (2 min):**
"Hi [Name], thanks for taking the time. I know you're busy, so let's jump in. 
When we chatted [via message], you mentioned [their problem]. Is that still the main thing you're dealing with?"

**Diagnosis (5 min):**
"Tell me about the current situation. What's the biggest impact this is having on your team?"
[Listen. Take notes.]

**Solution (5 min):**
"Here's what I'd do for you: [your product spec in plain language]. 
This would take [timeline] and cost €[price]. Does that feel like it would actually solve this?"

**Close (3 min):**
If they say yes: "Perfect. I'm excited to help. Let's get started. I'll send you an invoice today, and we'll kick off on [date]. Sound good?"

If they say maybe: "I get it—this is a decision. What information would help you decide? When would make sense to chat again?"

If they say no: "No worries. What would have to be true for this to work for you?" [Listen. Maybe pivot pricing/scope.]

**Closer (1 min):**
"Thanks for your time, [Name]. I'll [send invoice/follow up Monday/keep you posted]."
```

## Exercise 6: Gather Proof from Every Customer

**Time: 1–2 hours per customer (spread over 3–4 weeks)**

As customers complete deliveries, get:

1. **Testimonial** (3–5 sentences about working with you)
2. **Metrics** (what improved? by how much?)
3. **Case study outline** (optional but powerful)

Use the same request template from Phase 4:

```
Subject: Quick favor—your feedback would help

Hi [Name],

I'd love to hear how [product] worked for you. Could you spend 2 minutes answering:

1. What was the main problem you were facing?
2. How did [my solution] help?
3. What would you tell someone considering working with me?

[Optional:]
4. Any metrics? (time saved, cost reduced, performance improved, etc.)

—Thanks, [Your name]
```

**Save every testimonial.** You'll use these in:
- Your website/LinkedIn
- Sales calls ("I helped [similar company] do X")
- Content/blog posts
- Email marketing

## Common Mistakes in Phase 5

❌ **Not tracking metrics** — You can't improve what you don't measure.

❌ **Giving up too early** — It takes 3–4 weeks to cycle through the sales funnel. Don't quit after 5 messages.

❌ **Not following up** — Most sales happen on the 2nd or 3rd touch, not the first.

❌ **Changing your message too fast** — Give each message variation 15–20 tries before declaring it a winner.

❌ **Forgetting to ask for proof** — Do NOT move on without testimonials and metrics.
