**Estimated time to deliver (from your spec):**

```

```

**Your hourly rate (what you'd charge as a freelancer):**

```

```

**Cost calculation:**

```
Cost = (hours × hourly rate) ÷ 2

Why divide by 2? Because you'll get faster and more efficient after a few deliveries.
Your real cost will be lower once you have templates and processes.

Example:
- 8 hours × €100/hour = €800
- €800 ÷ 2 = €400 cost to deliver

```

## Step 4: Set Your Pricing

**The pricing formula:**

```
Your price = Cost × 2 to Cost × 3, but not higher than market rate, not lower than 10% of customer value
```

**Example:**

```
Cost to deliver: €400
Market rate: €1,500–2,500
Customer value: €5,000+

Recommended price: €800–1,200 (2–3x cost, well below market, huge value for customer)
```


## Step 6: The Psychology of Pricing

**Key principle:** You're not underpricing if you're getting paid AND getting testimonials.

**Founder pricing is NOT free:**

```
❌ Don't do it free. Free customers don't give testimonials.
✅ Do charge something. Even €500 is enough to get buy-in.
```

**Why you raise prices:**

```
Customer 1: €500 (founder price, invest in case study)
         ↓ (You now have case study + testimonial)
Customer 2: €750 (proof price, still good deal)
         ↓ (You have 2 customers + 2 testimonials)
Customer 3: €1,000 (approaching market rate)
         ↓ (You have 3 customers + social proof)
Customer 4: €1,500+ (full market rate, now you have proof)
```

## Step 8: Price Testing

**How will you test if your price is right?**

Track these metrics as you land customers:

```
Customer 1:
- Sold at price: €___
- Did they hesitate? [Yes/No]
- Would they have paid more? [Ask them after delivery]

Customer 2:
- Sold at price: €___
- Did they hesitate? [Yes/No]

Customer 3:
- Sold at price: €___
- Did they hesitate? [Yes/No]
```

**If they hesitate:**
- Your price might be too high for your proof level
- Consider offering a payment plan ("€1,000 or €600 + €400 in 30 days")
- Or include a bonus deliverable ("report + 2 follow-up calls")

---

## Notes

- **Don't overthink pricing.** You'll adjust it after your first few customers.
- **You can always lower price later if needed,** but harder to raise it.
- **Charge SOMETHING** (even €500) so customers take you seriously.
- **Your price will feel too low.** That's normal. Adjust upward with each customer.
