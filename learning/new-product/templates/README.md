- **[early-validation-worksheet.md](early-validation-worksheet.md)** — Your first 5 prospect conversations

## Phase 3: Design

---

## How to Use These Templates

1. **Make a copy** of any template
2. **Fill it in** with your specific info
3. **Use it repeatedly** (same template for every customer)
4. **Improve it** after each use (what would make this better?)

Templates aren't perfect. You'll customize them. That's the point.
