# Sales Call Script

**Phase:** 5 - Launch
**Time to complete:** 20–30 minutes per call

Your proven conversation script for closing sales.

---

## Pre-Call Checklist

Before you get on the call:

```
[ ] You know their company name and their role
[ ] You've re-read your earlier conversation with them
[ ] You have your product spec in front of you
[ ] You have 2 price points ready (in case they negotiate)
[ ] You have your calendar open to schedule the next step
[ ] You're in a quiet place (no background noise)
[ ] You have pen and paper (or laptop) to take notes
```

---

## Opening (2 minutes)

**Goal:** Confirm the call, set expectations, and re-establish rapport.

```
"Hi [Name], thanks so much for taking the time today. I know you're busy, so I'll be respectful of your time.

We've talked about [their problem]. Today, I want to:
1. Make sure I understand your situation correctly
2. Walk through how I'd help
3. See if this is a good fit

Sound good?"

[They say yes]

"Perfect. Let's start with you. Since we last talked, has [problem] changed at all? Still your main priority?"
```

---

## Diagnosis (5 minutes)

**Goal:** Confirm they still have the problem and understand the impact.

**Listen more than you talk.**

```
"Tell me about your current situation with [specific problem]. What's the biggest impact on your team?"

[Listen. Take notes. Don't interrupt.]

If they're unclear:
"So when you say [their words], do you mean [your interpretation]? Help me understand..."

Confirmation question:
"On a scale of 1–10, how important is solving this in the next [30/60 days]?"

If they say 7+: You're good. Move forward.
If they say <7: "What would make it a 10? What needs to change?" [Listen.]
```

---

## Solution (5–7 minutes)

**Goal:** Present your offer clearly and confidently.

**The script:**

```
"Based on what you've told me, here's what I propose:

[Deliverable 1]: [Plain language description]
[Deliverable 2]: [Plain language description]
[Deliverable 3]: [Plain language description]

The timeline is [X weeks/days], and here's how it works:

Week 1: [What happens]
Week 2: [What happens]
[etc.]

By the end, you'll have [specific outcome]. Our goal is [metric/result].

This would be [price] total.

Does that feel like it would actually solve what you're dealing with?"

[Stop and listen. Don't keep talking.]
```

---

## Objection Handling (2–3 minutes)

**If they say:**

### "It's expensive"

```
"I get it. Here's what's included: [list deliverables]. Most teams recover this cost within [timeframe] because [specific benefit].

What's your main concern—the total price, or the timeline? Let's talk through it."
```

### "I need to think about it"

```
"Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through?

[Listen to their concern]

Can I help clarify anything? Or would it help if we talked again [specific day] after you've had time to discuss with [their boss/team]?"
```

### "We don't have the budget right now"

```
"I understand. When do you expect to have clarity on budget? [Listen.]

We could also structure this as [payment plan option]. Would that help?"
```

### "Can you do it for less?"

```
"What price would work for you? [Listen.]

Here's the thing: this is [X hours] of work, and I deliver [specific value]. At [your price], I'm actually below market rate because I want to build a relationship and get a testimonial.

I could reduce scope instead: I could skip [deliverable], which would bring it to [lower price]. Would that work?"
```

---

## Soft Close (1–2 minutes)

**Goal:** Move toward a yes or identify the real objection.

```
"So here's what I'm hearing: you have [problem], you want [outcome], and [your solution] would get you there.

Does that feel right?"

[They say yes or bring up another objection]

If yes:
"Great. I'm excited to help. Are you ready to move forward?"

[If still hesitant: "What's holding you back?"]
```

---

## Hard Close (1 minute)

**When they say yes:**

```
"Perfect. I'm excited to work with you. Here's what happens next:

1. I'll send you an invoice today for [price]
2. Once payment clears, we'll schedule the kickoff call for [date]
3. I'll send you a prep questionnaire so I can get started immediately

Can you do payment by [date] so we can start on [start date]?"

[Confirm and take note of their email for invoice]

"Great. I'll get this to you in the next hour. Any questions before we hang up?"

[Answer questions]

"Perfect. Thanks again, [Name]. I'll be in touch."
```

---

## Post-Call Checklist

Immediately after the call:

```
[ ] Send invoice (use template)
[ ] Note their email and contact info
[ ] Add to your CRM/tracker
[ ] Schedule kickoff questionnaire to send
[ ] Calendar: kickoff call 3–5 days out
[ ] Write down what you learned (price reaction, objections, etc.)
```

---

## Notes on Delivery

**Key principles:**

✅ **Pause after you present.** Don't fill silence with more talking.
✅ **Listen more than you talk.** Your job is to understand, not convince.
✅ **Be honest about scope.** Don't oversell what you can do.
✅ **Offer payment options if they're hesitant.** (But don't go lower than 20% discount.)
✅ **Close with confidence.** If you believe in your offer, they will too.

## Common Call Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Over-explaining.** They don't need every detail. Summarize.
❌ **Not confirming the problem.** Make sure they still care.
❌ **Pitching features instead of outcomes.** They don't care what you do; they care what they get.
❌ **Not handling price objections.** Have 2–3 responses ready.
❌ **Not closing.** Ask for the sale. Directly.
❌ **Letting them leave without next steps.** "Let me think about it" means no. Get clarity.
