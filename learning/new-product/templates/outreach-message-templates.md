# Outreach Message Templates

**Phase:** 2 - Validation
**Time to complete:** 1 hour (test 3 variations)

Three proven message variations. Test all three. Use the one with the highest reply rate.

---

## Message Template 1: Problem-First

**Best for:** When the problem is obvious and they've likely thought about it

**Use this when:** They have a clear pain point visible from their profile/LinkedIn

```
Subject: Quick question about [their pain point]

Hi [Name],

I saw that you [specific observation about their company/work]. 

I've spent the last [X years/months] helping teams deal with [your problem area]. 

Quick question: when you [specific symptom of problem], what's usually the biggest challenge? [Pain point]?

Curious because I'm exploring this with a few teams right now.

— [Your name]
```

**Real Example:**

```
Subject: Question on Laravel performance

Hi Maria,

I saw your company recently posted about scaling your backend. I've spent the last 18 months optimizing APIs for agencies dealing with similar load issues.

Quick question: when your API response times spike, how long does it usually take your team to identify the bottleneck?

Curious because I'm talking to agencies about this specifically.

— Alex
```

---

## Message Template 2: Proof-First

**Best for:** When you have case studies or similar companies you can reference

**Use this when:** You want to show credibility early

```
Subject: [Company type] like yours + [problem solution]

Hi [Name],

I just helped [similar company] solve [specific problem they have]. They saw [metric result] in [timeframe].

Sounds like [Company] might be dealing with similar challenges. How are you handling [specific problem area]?

— [Your name]
```

**Real Example:**

```
Subject: API performance for agencies

Hi James,

I just helped TechCorp reduce their API response time by 60% in 10 days. They were a 15-person agency like yours, handling multiple client backends.

Sounds like Acme might be dealing with similar scaling issues. When your production APIs are slow, how long does it usually take to identify and fix the issue?

— Alex
```

---

## Message Template 3: Referral-Based

**Best for:** When you have a mutual connection or an existing customer in their network

**Use this when:** Someone referred you or you can mention a customer from their industry

```
Subject: [Referrer name] recommended I reach out

Hi [Name],

[Existing customer/mutual connection] mentioned that your team might be dealing with [problem]. He had great results working with me on [solution].

Might be worth 10 minutes to see if there's a fit. What do you think?

— [Your name]
```

**Real Example:**

```
Subject: Marcus from AgencyXYZ recommended I reach out

Hi Chris,

Marcus mentioned that your team is navigating similar technical challenges around [performance/deployment/etc]. He had great results optimizing his API architecture.

Might be worth a quick conversation. Free?

— Alex
```

---

## How to Personalize Each Message

Before sending, customize:

1. **[Name]** — Use their actual first name
2. **[Specific observation]** — One sentence about THEM (not generic)
3. **[Pain point]** — Specific to their company (not generic)
4. **[Timeframe]** — Your actual background
5. **[Metric/Result]** — Real numbers from your work

**Do NOT send the template as-is.** Always personalize.

## Response Handling

**If they reply:**

```
"Thanks for getting back to me. So when you mention [their problem], do you mean [specific symptom]?"

[Listen to their answer]

"Would you be open to a quick 15-min call to chat about how you're handling this? I might have some thoughts."
```

**If they don't reply in 5 days:**

```
Quick follow-up (ONE ONLY):

"Hey [Name], following up on my note from [day]. I know you're busy—quick question if you have 30 seconds: [your question about their problem]?"
```

**If they say "maybe" or "sounds interesting":**

```
"I get it—worth thinking through. What would help you decide? Happy to share more context."
```

## Pro Tips

✅ Keep it short (3–4 sentences max)
✅ Ask a question (don't pitch)
✅ Personalize (mention something specific about THEM)
✅ Be genuine (why are you really asking?)
✅ Space out messages (don't spam all 30 at once)
✅ Follow up once (if no reply after 5 days)
✅ Track everything (so you know what works)

❌ Don't pitch your solution in the first message
❌ Don't include links to your website
❌ Don't ask for a call immediately
❌ Don't send the same message to everyone
❌ Don't follow up more than once
