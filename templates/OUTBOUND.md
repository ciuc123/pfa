Outbound research & daily workflow

Objective: research 10–15 warm prospects/day using buying signals and reach out with human, researched messaging.

1) Research process (10–20 minutes per prospect initially)
- Source list: LinkedIn search, Sales Navigator, Twitter, company news, Crunchbase
- Confirm role, company size, geography
- Identify buying signals: funding, headcount growth, product launches, leadership changes
- Capture the signal in the tracking sheet and the exact line to reference in the message

2) Daily cadence
- Morning (60–90 minutes): research & send 10–15 connection requests / targeted messages
- Afternoon (20–30 minutes): follow-ups and replies; calendar management
- End of day (10 minutes): update tracking sheet and note 3 learnings

3) Messaging rules
- Personalize the first line to the buying signal
- Keep tone human: curiosity + value, not salesy
- If prospect replies, move to qualification, ask calendar-friendly meeting times, and update tracking sheet

4) Qualification framework (first call aim)
- 3 questions: (1) budget/authority, (2) impact needed (metric), (3) timing
- If fit: propose a short 20–30 minute discovery meeting with clear next step

5) Cadence (example)
- Day 0: Connection request or short message referencing signal
- Day 3: Follow-up (value / question)
- Day 7: Short case / quick ask to book 15–20 minute call
- Day 14: Final check-in (no pressure, keeps door open)

6) Tools & trackers
- Use `templates/TRACKING_SHEET.csv` to record all activity
- Use Calendly or equivalent for booking
- Optional: simple CRM or Notion database

7) Hiring an appointment setter (if you delegate)
- Document research steps and message scripts
- Create a short training script and QA process (first 50 prospects reviewed by you)

8) Minimum daily inputs to expect
- 10–15 researched prospects
- 30 minutes of your time daily for engagement and replies (or hire to do replies)
