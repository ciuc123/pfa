30-day content calendar and hooks (starter)

Goal: a content funnel that pre-sells, answers objections, and pulls prospects inbound.

Structure
- Week 1: Positioning & Offer clarity
- Week 2: Buying signals & counter-objections
- Week 3: Case studies & small tactical wins
- Week 4: Conversations & CTA-driven posts

Posting cadence
- 3 posts/week minimum (e.g., Tue/Thu/Sat). Add short-form video once/week.

Sample hooks & post ideas (templates)

Week 1
- Post 1: "Why 99% of LinkedIn outreach fails: you're in 5th gear — here's the repair manual for first gear." (explain concept)
- Post 2: Offer short explainer: "If you’re Head of Marketing at B2B SaaS, here’s the one sentence I want you to read." (one-liner)
- Post 3: Short proof: quick client stat + call to DM for an audit

Week 2
- Post 4: "3 buying signals that mean a prospect is ready to talk (and where to find them)." (educational)
- Post 5: Objection post: "We tried LinkedIn before" — why that’s not the real problem
- Video: 60–90s camera take summarizing a small play

Week 3
- Post 6: Case study (before/after numbers). Keep it short and measurable.
- Post 7: Tactics: "How I turn a job ad into a 3-line outreach that books a meeting."
- Post 8: Quick checklist: what to look at when auditing your LinkedIn profile

Week 4
- Post 9: Live lessons from weekly reviews (what’s landing / what failed)
- Post 10: CTA: book a 15-minute audit / reply with the last thing they tried
- Video: Client testimonial or walkthrough of the 2-step process

Post format rules
- Hook in first line, then 3–5 short paragraphs/bullets, end with CTA (DM, comment, or calendar link).
- For video, make the first 2–3 seconds explain the outcome.

Repurposing
- Turn each post into a short LinkedIn text post, a 60–90s video script, and 2–3 tweet-sized lines for syndication.
