## What this strategy does

Targets small business owners (3-50 people) with dormant email lists (100-1,000 subscribers) who aren't using email to generate revenue.

**You deliver:**
- 4-5 high-converting email campaigns (AI-assisted)
- Email platform setup and segmentation
- Revenue tracking and attribution
- Handoff playbook for ongoing use

**You get paid:**
- €500-1,500 upfront (per sprint)
- 10-15% revenue share for 12 months

**Timeline:** 4 weeks per client, 20-30 hours effort

## Where this fits in your business

This is a new buyer group (small business owners, not technical teams) and a new service type (marketing/revenue, not code/infrastructure). 

**Stack with your backend audits:**
- Backend audits: technical founders, €750-1,500, one-time
- Email list growth: all small business owners, €500-1,500 + ongoing, repeatable

Multiple revenue streams targeting the same customer base (small businesses) with different entry points.
