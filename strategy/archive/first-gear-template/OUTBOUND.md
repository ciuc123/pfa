Outbound SOP — exact, timeboxed playbook (use for first 30 prospects)

Objective: a repeatable SOP that produces 10–15 qualified prospect outreaches/day and yields testable signals (reply rate & meetings). This is a 3-day research + 27-day execution plan.

Preparation (30–60 minutes, one time)

- Create a saved Sales Navigator search or LinkedIn search using the boolean examples below.
- Open `templates/TRACKING_SHEET.csv` in Sheets/Excel and make a copy for this campaign.

Boolean search examples (Sales Navigator / LinkedIn)

- Role: "(Head of Marketing OR "VP Growth" OR "Growth Lead")"
- Company size: "(11..50 OR 51..200)"
- Industry: "B2B SaaS"
- Example combined: Role AND Industry AND Company size -> use Sales Nav filters to mirror this.

Daily workflow (timeboxed - for you or a VA)

Day 0–2: Research sprint (3 days)

Each day (90–120 minutes total)
- 0–60 min: Research and add 10 prospects to the tracking sheet (capture the exact buying signal line and a one-sentence reason to contact)
- 60–90 min: Send first messages (connection requests or short messages) to those 10
- 90–120 min: Update tracking sheet with templates used, estimated research time and a short 1-line hypothesis

Day 3 onward: steady cadence (60–90 minutes/day)

- 0–45 min: Research & send 10 new prospects (capture signal) OR follow-up with replies
- 45–60 min: Triage replies and book meetings; send follow-ups to non-repliers as per sequence
- 60–75 min: Log data and 3 learnings for the day

Research checklist (fill every prospect row)

- Confirm name, role, company URL
- Copy exact line for signal (job ad headline, funding announcement, press quote)
- One-sentence personalization to use in message
- Estimated research time (mins)

Message hygiene & testing

- A/B test subject lines/messages in batches of 30. Keep variables minimal: first-line signal vs first-line pain.
- Track which template version produced replies in the tracking sheet.

Qualification framework (call objective)

Ask 3 calibrated questions on the first call:
1) Outcome: "What would success look like in the next 90 days?" (metric)
2) Capacity: "Who owns pipeline today and who signs off on budget?"
3) Timing: "If this maps, when could you move forward?"

Follow-up cadence (exact sequence — use LinkedIn messages or email)

- Day 0: Connection / short signal-based message
- Day 3: Follow-up 1 (value or micro-ask)
- Day 7: Follow-up 2 (case + meeting ask)
- Day 14: Final check-in (leave the door open)

Hiring + delegation (quick SOP for an appointment setter)

- Provide the VA/ASA with the boolean search, a 3-step research checklist and 5 message templates.
- QA: review the first 50 prospects they add; give written feedback and 10 corrected examples.

Metrics to record every week

- Prospects added
- Messages sent
- Replies received (reply %)
- Meetings booked
- Meetings → qualified

3-day test plan (run this before scaling)

1) Day 0–2: Research 30 prospects and send 30 first messages.
2) Day 3–10: Run follow-ups and triage replies.
3) End of week 2: Review tracking sheet and pick the top-performing message & ICP. Repeat with adjusted ICP for next 30.
