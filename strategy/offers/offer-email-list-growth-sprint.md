# Offer: AI-Powered Email List Growth Sprint

## Who it's for

A founder, marketing manager, or owner of a small business (3–50 people) that has a customer email list but isn't actively using it to generate revenue — either because they lack time, marketing skills, or confidence in their messaging — and wants to test whether regular email campaigns can drive sales without hiring a full-time marketer.

**Best-fit verticals:**
- E-commerce shops (product-based)
- SaaS founders (product updates, feature releases, upgrades)
- Service providers: consulting, design, coaching, training (course launches, workshop signups)
- Digital product creators: templates, courses, tools
- Local services: fitness, beauty, coaching, consulting

## What pain it solves

- "We have 500+ emails but send nothing. We're leaving money on the table."
- "We tried email campaigns but they felt salesy and nobody opened them."
- "We don't have time to write compelling emails or segment our list."
- "We're not sure what to say or when to say it — we keep procrastinating."
- "We want to sell more but can't afford a full-time marketer or agency."
- "Our competitors send emails weekly. We send nothing."

## Observable buying signals

- Founder or team member with an active email list (newsletter signup, past purchases, webinar attendees)
- Recent product launch, course, or new service offering they want to promote
- LinkedIn post about growing their business or customer base
- Website footer showing newsletter signup but low activity
- Founder asking publicly: "How do you stay in touch with customers?" or "What's your email strategy?"
- Recent launch or pivot needing customer awareness
- Team small/lean (no dedicated marketing hire)

## What the client gets

1. **Email Strategy Audit** — current list size, segment analysis, past send history
2. **5 Done-For-You Campaign Templates** — high-converting email sequences for their business type
3. **AI-Assisted Copy Writing** — headlines, body copy, and CTAs tailored to their audience
4. **Segmentation Plan** — how to categorize their list by customer type, purchase history, or engagement
5. **Send Schedule** — optimal days/times and frequency (weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly based on their goals)
6. **Email Tool Setup** — integration with their ESP (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, SendGrid, Klaviyo, Brevo)
7. **A/B Testing Framework** — how to test subject lines and content, track opens/clicks/conversions
8. **Performance Dashboard** — simple tracking for opens, clicks, unsubscribes, revenue attribution
9. **30-Day Campaign Launch** — you draft, they review and send; 4 emails sent over the sprint
10. **Follow-up Playbook** — one-page guide so they can keep sending emails after the sprint ends

## Revenue Model: Performance-Based Partnership

This offer is different from typical consulting. You are offering a **hybrid model**:

### Option 1: Starter Service (Fixed Fee + Revenue Share)
- **Upfront fee:** €500–800 for strategy, copy, segmentation, setup
- **Revenue share:** 10% of revenue attributed to your emails for 12 months after sprint ends
- **Engagement level:** 4–5 emails written during 4-week sprint; they send and manage day-to-day

### Option 2: Managed Service (Retainer + Revenue Share)
- **Monthly retainer:** €400–600 (you write 2–4 emails per month)
- **Revenue share:** 15% of revenue attributed to your campaigns for the duration of engagement
- **Engagement level:** You manage ongoing copy, segmentation, and optimization; they approve and send

### Option 3: Full Performance-Based (No Upfront Fee)
- **Revenue share:** 20% of revenue attributed to your emails for 12 months
- **Engagement level:** Full 4-week sprint; you write all copy; you manage launch
- **Risk:** Only suitable if you trust their product/market fit and they have revenue already

**Why this works:**
- The client is not paying a large upfront cost — lower risk for them
- You get paid based on results, which aligns your incentive with theirs
- Performance-based revenue can exceed fixed fees if the emails convert well
- Creates ongoing relationship (you track revenue for months/years)
- Builds case studies with real ROI metrics

## What you actually do (delivery methodology)

### Week 1: Discovery + Audit

**Day 1–2: Deep Discovery Call**
1. Schedule 60-minute call with founder/marketing manager
2. Ask:
   - What are they selling? (products, services, courses, subscriptions?)
   - What is their current revenue? (this determines conversion targets)
   - Who are their ideal customers? (what problems do they solve for?)
   - Email list size and source (purchases, newsletter signup, webinar, lead magnet?)
   - Have they sent emails before? (if yes: what worked? what didn't?)
   - When did they last touch their list?
   - What is their goal for the sprint? (10 sales, €2k revenue, X% open rate?)
   - Do they have an ESP? (email service provider)

**Day 3–4: Audit Their List and Past Campaigns**
1. Get access to their ESP (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Klaviyo, etc.)
2. Analyze:
   - List size and growth rate
   - Segment data (if any): paid customers vs. newsletter subscribers, product categories, geography
   - Historical send data: past open rates, click rates, unsubscribe rates (if they've sent before)
   - Compare their metrics to industry benchmarks:
     - E-commerce: 20–30% open rate, 2–5% click rate
     - SaaS: 25–35% open rate, 3–7% click rate
     - Services: 30–40% open rate, 5–10% click rate
3. Document what's working (if anything) and gaps

**Day 5: Build Email Strategy Document**
1. Create a one-page strategy covering:
   - List segments (who are we emailing? group them by buyer type)
   - Email goals for the sprint (primary: revenue; secondary: engagement/opens)
   - Target metrics (realistic based on their list and industry)
   - Campaign themes (what are we promoting? product launches, discounts, education, etc.)
   - Send schedule (frequency: weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly?)
   - Revenue attribution plan (how will we track which emails drove sales?)

### Week 2: Copy + Template Development

**Day 6–7: Research + Framework**
1. Visit their website: product pages, past landing pages, customer testimonials
2. Study their brand voice: LinkedIn posts, emails they've sent, customer reviews
3. Identify their unique value: why would someone buy from them vs. competitors?
4. Download 5–10 high-performing emails from their industry (Mailchimp templates, competitor benchmarking)

**Day 8–9: AI-Assisted Copy Creation**
1. For each campaign (typically 4–5 emails for the sprint), create:
   - **Subject line** (2–3 variants): Test which performs better
   - **Hook** (first line): Curiosity, pain point, or benefit
   - **Body** (150–300 words): Story, social proof, or education
   - **CTA** (call to action): Clear next step (buy, sign up, watch demo, book call)
2. Prompt examples for Claude or ChatGPT:
   ```
   "Write a welcome email for a fitness coach's list who just signed up for her free 7-day challenge. 
   Tone: warm, personal, non-salesy. 
   Goal: get them to start the challenge today.
   Include: her story, why the challenge works, and one social proof testimonial."
   
   "Write a launch email for a digital product (€47 course on email marketing). 
   Audience: small business owners, no marketing background. 
   Tone: helpful and urgent (launch ends in 3 days). 
   Include: what they'll learn, a testimonial, and FAQs as bullet points."
   ```
3. Use tools: Claude, ChatGPT, or Copywriting frameworks like AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action) or Problem-Agitate-Solve
4. Create email templates in their ESP (Mailchimp template, ConvertKit, etc.)

**Day 10: Segmentation Setup**
1. Tag their subscribers:
   - By segment (customer type, product purchased, engagement level)
   - By status (active, inactive, VIP, at-risk)
2. Create segments in their ESP:
   - "Recent customers" (bought in last 90 days) — send product-focused emails
   - "Email subscribers only" — send educational/nurture emails
   - "Inactive" (no open in 6+ months) — send re-engagement email
   - "VIP customers" (highest lifetime value) — exclusive offers
3. Document the logic so they can maintain it

### Week 3: Testing + Optimization

**Day 11–12: A/B Testing Framework**
1. Design 2–3 A/B tests for the sprint:
   - Subject line variants (curiosity vs. benefit vs. direct)
   - Send time (Tuesday morning vs. Thursday afternoon)
   - CTA placement (middle vs. bottom of email)
2. Set up tracking in their ESP:
   - Enable click tracking on all links
   - Tag revenue-generating links (if possible with UTM parameters or Zapier automation)
3. Create simple tracking spreadsheet:
   ```
   | Email | Send Date | Segment | Subject | Open % | Click % | Revenue | ROI |
   |-------|-----------|---------|---------|--------|---------|---------|-----|
   | Campaign 1 | Jan 8 | All | Line A | 28% | 4.2% | €150 | 2x |
   ```

**Day 13–14: Dashboard Setup**
1. Set up simple tracking (choose one):
   - **Spreadsheet-based** (simplest): Manual log of opens, clicks, revenue from their analytics
   - **Zapier integration** (medium): Zapier triggers when email link is clicked → logs to Google Sheet
   - **ESP native** (best): Use Mailchimp/Klaviyo native reporting + UTM parameters in links
2. Create a simple visual dashboard showing:
   - Total emails sent
   - Average open rate and click rate
   - Revenue attributed to campaigns
   - Unsubscribe rate
3. Explain how to read it: "This shows which emails drive the most revenue per click"

### Week 4: Launch + Handoff

**Day 15–16: Draft 4–5 Campaigns**
1. For their business type, write ready-to-send campaigns:
   - Campaign 1: Welcome/Value (if new list) or Re-engagement (if dormant list)
   - Campaign 2: Educational/Nurture (why your approach works, social proof)
   - Campaign 3: Promotional/Launch (product offer, limited time, scarcity)
   - Campaign 4: Follow-up/Urgency (closing the sale, last chance)
   - Campaign 5 (optional): Upsell to existing customers or referral request
2. Each email: subject line + copy + images (if needed) + CTA

**Day 17–19: Client Review + Iteration**
1. Send drafts to client for review (give them 2–3 day turnaround)
2. Collect feedback: tone, offers, timing, segments
3. Iterate (typically 2 rounds of feedback)
4. Get written approval before sending

**Day 20: Send Campaign 1**
1. Load into ESP with correct segment
2. A/B test subject lines (50% get variant A, 50% get variant B)
3. Send at optimal time (based on their past data or industry average)
4. Monitor: open rate, click rate, unsubscribes in first 2 hours

**Day 21–22: Monitor + Send Campaign 2**
1. Review Campaign 1 results after 24 hours
2. Document: opens, clicks, revenue
3. Send Campaign 2 (2–5 days after Campaign 1, depending on frequency)

**Day 23–28: Continue Cadence**
1. Send remaining campaigns on schedule
2. Monitor each one for opens, clicks, revenue
3. Make small adjustments (send time, segmentation) based on performance

**Day 29–30: Handoff + Training**
1. Compile final results:
   - Total opens, clicks, revenue
   - Open rates vs. industry benchmarks
   - Which subject lines won
   - Which segments engaged most
2. Create one-page "Email Playbook":
   ```
   # Your Email Playbook (Keep Using This)
   
   ## Best performing content types:
   - Subject lines with curiosity: 35% open rate
   - Educational emails: 4.8% click rate
   - Limited-time offers: 12% conversion rate
   
   ## Optimal send schedule:
   - Tuesday at 9 AM (best for your audience)
   - Frequency: 1x per week
   
   ## Next 30 days (your homework):
   - Send 4 more campaigns using the templates we created
   - Test 2 new subject line approaches (try questions vs. numbers)
   - Segment new signups from your checkout form
   - Track revenue in the dashboard
   
   ## When to email:
   - New product launch: email active customers first
   - Seasonal event: email 3x per week (high volume)
   - Educational content: email 2x per month (nurture)
   
   ## Tools we used:
   - [Your ESP] for sending
   - [UTM parameters / Zapier] for tracking
   - [Spreadsheet link] for the dashboard
   ```
3. Bonus: 30-minute training call where you walk through:
   - How to write more emails using the templates
   - How to read the dashboard
   - How to track new campaigns
   - What to do if open rates drop

## Frequently Asked Questions

### "How do you track revenue attribution?"
Answer: We'll use either:
1. **UTM parameters** on links (you add `?source=email_campaign_1` to your links) — shows which campaign drove the visit
2. **Zapier automation** (when email link is clicked, we log it to a spreadsheet)
3. **ESP native tracking** (Klaviyo and Mailchimp have built-in conversion tracking)
4. **Manual customer interviews** (I ask: "How did you hear about us?" when they buy)

Start with UTM parameters — it's the simplest and most reliable.

### "What if email performance is low?"
Answer: We'll iterate. Low performance usually means one of:
1. **Subject line isn't compelling** — we'll A/B test new ones
2. **Audience segmentation is wrong** — we might email the wrong people
3. **Offer isn't strong** — we might need to improve what you're selling
4. **Timing is off** — we might email on the wrong day

The playbook will guide you on how to diagnose and fix this.

### "What if people unsubscribe?"
Answer: Some unsubscribes are normal (1–2%). Unsubscribe rate only becomes a problem if it's 5%+, which means:
- You're sending too frequently
- You're sending to the wrong audience
- Your messaging doesn't match their expectations

We'll monitor this and adjust.

### "Can you do this for a product that's not ready yet?"
Answer: No. Email marketing works best when you have a proven offer. If your product is still in development, we should focus on building a waitlist first, then email the waitlist when you launch.

### "What if I only have 50 subscribers?"
Answer: It's risky to pay for this service with fewer than 100 subscribers (hard to measure results). But you could:
1. Build your list to 100+ first (we can help create a lead magnet strategy)
2. After you have 150+ emails, hire me for the sprint

### "Can you keep managing email after the sprint?"
Answer: Yes. We can shift to Option 2 (managed service retainer) where I write and optimize emails monthly. Cost: €400–800/month depending on volume + 10–15% revenue share.
